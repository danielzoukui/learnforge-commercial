Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes. You have full self-service control through your Stripe Customer Portal. You can cancel with one click at any time, and you will retain access until the end of your paid billing period. There are no cancellation fees or tricky phone calls.
Are there annual contracts or hidden commitments?
No. All LearnForge self-service plans are strictly month-to-month ($3/mo for Family, $5/mo for Teacher). No long-term lock-in and no annual upfront commitments.
How does student privacy work?
LearnForge is built with a local-first architecture. Core student learning data and progress remain stored right in the device's browser. Server subscriptions only maintain your billing status and entitlement credentials. We never sell student information or track students across the web.
Why is School / District self-service purchase disabled?
Institutional school deployments require customized data-processing agreements, FERPA and COPPA compliance protocols, and designated district administrators. In accordance with LearnForge commercial guidelines, institutional tiers are provisioned directly with our enterprise team.
