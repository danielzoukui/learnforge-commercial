Privacy

Launch draft: this page is a product placeholder and must be reviewed against the final data flows, vendors, child-account model, and applicable law before paid public launch.

Current app behavior

The deployed LearnForge learning app stores learning progress and preferences in the browser unless a hosted commercial service is explicitly connected. Commercial account and subscription services, when enabled, store account, subscription, entitlement, and audit records in the production backend.

Data controls

Use LearnForge’s in-app Privacy & Data center to review and export browser-local LearnForge data. Hosted-account export and deletion workflows must be enabled and tested before paid launch.

Children and schools

LearnForge is designed for learners, families, teachers, and schools. The final production launch must implement the consent, school authorization, retention, deletion, and vendor terms required for the deployment context.
